Privacy Policy

Application: AI Study Companion (com.sivakrishna.aistudycompanion)
Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: AI Study Companion is designed with a mobile-first, privacy-respecting architecture. Your uploaded PDF documents, notes, chat transcripts, and quiz results are stored locally on your device storage by default.

1. Information We Collect and Process

When you use AI Study Companion, we interact with the following categories of information to provide the application's core educational features:

  • Study Documents (PDFs): When you choose to import documents (lecture notes, past papers, syllabus), the files are copied to your device's sandboxed local storage directory. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your study materials.
  • User-Generated Study Data: Interactive study chats, quiz scores, flashcards, and revision notes created within the app are saved locally on your device via AsyncStorage and native device storage.
  • Diagnostic and Device Information: Standard, non-identifiable technical data (e.g., OS version, device model, crash telemetry) may be processed to diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

2. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI Processing)

To provide features like document question answering, citations, summarization, and quiz generation, the app interfaces with the Google Gemini API (Google Cloud Generative AI):

  • When you submit a study question or request a summary, relevant excerpts or pages of your document are securely sent via encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini.
  • Data transmitted for AI processing is utilized solely to generate your requested response in real time.
  • Google's enterprise Generative AI privacy terms stipulate that customer prompt data submitted through official developer tiers is not used to train foundational models without consent.

3. Third-Party Services and Subprocessors

AI Study Companion integrates with trusted industry partners strictly for core app functionality:

  • Google Generative AI (Gemini API): Natural language understanding, document summarization, and quiz generation. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery, in-app updates, and Google Play Store licensing.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Local Storage: Your documents, revision sheets, and chat histories remain stored locally in your device's secure application sandbox.
  • Data Deletion: You have full control over your data. Deleting a document inside the app immediately deletes the local file and all associated chat logs. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local app data from your device.

5. Permissions Requested

  • Storage / Document Picker: Required only when you explicitly select and import a PDF file from your device or cloud storage drive.
  • Internet Access: Required to communicate with the Gemini AI service over secure HTTPS.

6. Children's Privacy

AI Study Companion is intended for high school, university, and adult professional students. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us for immediate removal.

7. Security Practices

All data transmitted outside the device (such as AI queries) uses industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption to prevent unauthorized interception. API credentials, where configured, are kept in secure application memory.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
